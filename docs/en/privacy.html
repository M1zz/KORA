Privacy Policy

Effective September 19, 2026 · Applies to app version 1.0.5 and later

This policy explains how the iOS app “KORA Korea Wayfinder” (the “app”), provided by the KORA developer (Leeo, “we”), handles information. Our principle is simple: process as little as possible, and keep it on your device.

At a glance

  • No sign-up or login. We don't collect your name, phone number, email or anything else that directly identifies you.
  • We don't run any servers. Your app settings are stored only on your device.
  • Location, camera, microphone, speech recognition and motion data are processed on your device and are never stored or sent off it.
  • We collect anonymous usage statistics with Google Analytics for Firebase to improve the app. There are no ads, we don't use the advertising identifier (IDFA), and we don't track you across other apps.

1. Information we process and how

a. Stored only on your device

So the app can pick up where you left off, the following is saved in the app's storage on your device (UserDefaults). It is never sent to us or anyone else.

  • The current (starting) station and destination you selected
  • Your display language
  • The region you selected in the station picker

b. Processed on your device only, never stored or sent

c. Usage statistics collected automatically (Google Analytics for Firebase)

When you use the app, Google Analytics for Firebase automatically collects:

  • An app instance ID (a random identifier created for each installation)
  • Usage events such as first launch (first_open), session start (session_start) and screen views
  • App version, device model, OS version and device language
  • Country and region estimated from your IP address (Google does not give us the IP address itself)

This information does not identify you, and we do not combine it with other data to identify you. We do not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA), we do not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, and the data is not used for advertising or for tracking you across other companies' apps or websites.

d. Real-time subway arrival data

To refine your current station during a ride, the app requests arrival information from the real-time subway arrival API of the Seoul Open Data Plaza (Seoul Metropolitan Government, data.seoul.go.kr). Only the station name is sent; no information that identifies you is included. As with any internet request, your IP address may reach the Seoul Metropolitan Government's servers, where it is handled under their own policies.

2. Why we process it

  • To provide the app's features: route guidance, current-station tracking and stop alerts
  • To remember your stations and language for the next launch
  • To understand how the app is used, fix bugs and improve features (statistics)

We never use it for advertising, marketing or profiling.

3. How long we keep it

  • Data stored on your device: deleted when you delete the app. We don't use iCloud sync. (It may be included in a full iPhone backup as part of your device data.)
  • Data processed on your device: discarded immediately after processing; never kept.
  • Usage statistics: deleted automatically according to Google Analytics' data retention setting (2 or 14 months). Aggregated statistics that cannot identify anyone may remain afterwards.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not provide your personal information to third parties, except where required by law.

7. Deletion

Data stored on your device is removed by iOS when you delete the app. Usage statistics are deleted automatically by Google after the retention period in section 3. We keep no separate copy of your information.

8. Your rights

  • You can revoke location, camera, microphone, speech recognition and motion access at any time in iPhone Settings.
  • Deleting the app erases everything stored on your device; reinstalling creates a new app instance ID.
  • To request access, correction, deletion or suspension of processing, email us at the address below and we will respond without delay. Because we hold no information that identifies you, we may not be able to locate a specific person's statistics.
  • You may also exercise these rights through a legal guardian or an authorized representative.

9. Cookies and similar technologies

The app does not use web cookies. Google Analytics for Firebase distinguishes usage statistics by the app instance ID described in 1-c. If you don't want this, you can delete the app.

10. Security

  • We process only the minimum necessary, and all sensitive processing happens on your device.
  • All network communication uses encrypted HTTPS.
  • We have no servers or databases, so no user data accumulates on our side.

11. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (under 14 in Korea), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
